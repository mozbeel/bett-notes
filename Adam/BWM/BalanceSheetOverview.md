### deprecatable Definition

deprecatable = loss of value, 99.9% of assets, in booking
Non-deprecatable = real-estate/assets on the ground

deprecation = value of item / years of deprecation (given by law) * years of usage

### How to deprecate fixed-assets

**normal deprecation**

When selling and price of value goes down,
once the reason for the price lowering doesn't exist anymore,
value can be put up back to the original price

**extraordinary deprecation**

booked with a different account.
one time loss, not yearly.
e.g. car crash -> repairing car -> not a new car = value goes down
