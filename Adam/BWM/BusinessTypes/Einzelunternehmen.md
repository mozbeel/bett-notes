#BWM #Business
- A sole trader can not have partners, but can have employees.

- You dont have to register in the Firmenbuch until you make more than 1 mil in a year.

- Tax scales based on income. Up to 55% at 1 mil in a year.

**Pro's**

- easy to start, no legal minimum capital requirement

- you run the company, get to decide

- all profits are for you

**Con's**

- you are liable and responsible

- it is hard to bring money into the company. (e.g. Loans, Startup, etc.)

- No one can take over management workload in case of sickness.

- Required being capable of getting business license to do certain work.
