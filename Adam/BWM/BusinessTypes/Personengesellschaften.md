#BWM #Business 
**Cooperation (OG=Unlimited Partnership)**

- minimum 2 fully liable people

- contract signed describing rights of each person.

- OG has to be in Company Register (Firmenbuch).

**Pro's**

- easy to startup

- easy to finance

- no minimum capital required

- can split up work between partners

- only one partner needs to meet requirements for a business card

**Con's**

- unlimited liability for yourself and partner.

- decision conflicts between partners

- partners are not allowed to run the same business.

**Example**

if partner is in debt, bank can come to you to
claim the debt, because you are liable for your partner.
