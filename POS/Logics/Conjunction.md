#POS #Logic #Logics 

- Boolean operation (junction) for linking two sentences (terms)
- example: this drink consists of wine and this drink consists of water
- true results only if both terms are true

| a   | b   | a **∧** b |
| --- | --- | --------- |
| t   | t   | t         |
| t   | f   | f         |
| f   | t   | f         |
| f   | f   | f         |
