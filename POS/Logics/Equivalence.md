(Boolean equality, equals) <->

boolean operatoin (junction) for linking two sentences (terms)
Example: his behaviour equals hers
True results only if a and b are equal

| a   | b   | a <-> b |
| --- | --- | ------- |
| t   | t   | t       |
| t   | f   | f       |
| f   | t   | f       |
| f   | f   | t       |
