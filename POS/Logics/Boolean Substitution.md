#POS #Logics #Logic 
- A boolean variable is substitutable by another boolean term
- Substitutions of tautological parts by other tautologies create tautologies
- substitutions of contradictional parts by other contradictions create contradictions


Notation
- a[p/b] every variable p in term a is substituted by term b


a: p -> q -> p
b: (r v s)
a [p/b]: (r v s) -> q -> (r v s)
