#BWM #Accounting #P23 #Important
UGB for businesses:
- Grundsatz der Blinanzstetigkeit: you have to stay with method selected, only change if you have good reasons
- Grundsatz der Unternehmensfortfuehrung: with the idea of the company  going on next year
- Grundssatz der Einzelbewertung: try assess each good and position and customer themselves
- Grundsatz der Bilanzvorsicht: being careful: we always have to assess ourselves poorer rather than richer, can't make yourself even 1€ richer. to protect the customer and the investors
- Grundsatz der Periodenabgrenzung: 1 business years, you're allowed to take the business cases that you can do in this year
- Grundsatz der Bilanzgleichheit: closing balance sheet has to be identical with the opening balance sheet of the next year
