Assets:
- Depcretable Asset: 99.9% of assets
- Non-deprecatable Asset: the ground (real-estatee)

How to deprecate non-deprecatable:
- Will stay with their initial buying value: not allowed to raise
- However: when the land gets less value: e.g.: chemical industry -> value will go down permanently, roads get built for 3 months -> temporarily lower, factory shuts down after 3 years -> temporarily gets lower, can get up to original buying price

How to calculate annual deprecation: (P37)
- buying price / time of use in years
- Buy for 40,000$ you intend using it for 10 years, annual deprecation: 40,000/10 = 4,000
