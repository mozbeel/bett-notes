#BWM #Business #P29
How many partners? 0. You can employ people. No business partners

Pros:
- Doesn't have to register in the [[Company Register]]
- All profit belongs to you
- ESt (Einkommensteuer, EStG Einkommensteuergesetz, income tax law): progressive way, % will raise the more income you make
	=> 1m dollars a year -> 55% taxation
- Easy and quick to start
Cons:
- Raising capital: can bring your own funds/capital, you can borrow an unlimited amount of money. Bank borrowing: no security for the bank, so they rarily can help -> difficult to raise capital 
- No shareholders allowed
- Liability: one person fully liable and in charge
	- e.g.: in debt for a million dollars -> fully liable
