# Kommanditgesellschaft (KG)

**English:** Limited Partnership

A **Kommanditgesellschaft (KG)** is a partnership with at least **two people who have different levels of liability**.

## Structure

A KG requires at least:

- **1 Komplementär (general partner)** — has **unlimited liability** and manages the business.
    
- **1 Kommanditist (limited partner)** — has **limited liability**, generally restricted to the amount they have contributed.
    

The **Komplementär** is responsible for running and representing the company. The **Kommanditist** primarily contributes capital and does not have the same management authority.

The KG must be **registered in the Company Register**.

## Financing

A KG can be attractive for financing because not all partners have unlimited personal liability. The presence of limited-liability partners can make financing more accessible, although the exact conditions depend on the business and the lender.

## Profit Distribution

Profits are distributed according to the **partnership agreement**.

## Taxation

The KG itself is generally not treated as the final taxpayer on its profits. Instead, profits are attributed to the individual partners and taxed according to the applicable rules.

## Reporting

Compared with corporations, a KG generally has **fewer public disclosure requirements**, although specific accounting and reporting obligations can still apply depending on its circumstances.

## Advantages

- **No minimum capital requirement**
    
- **Fast and relatively inexpensive to establish**
    
- Can make **financing easier** than for some other small-business structures
    
- **Limited liability** for the Kommanditists
    
- Different roles allow one partner to manage the business while others mainly provide capital
    

## Disadvantages

- At least one partner has **unlimited personal liability**
    
- Partners can face **restrictions on competing businesses**
    
- **Kommanditists generally do not have ordinary management authority**
    
- The different roles and levels of liability can create potential for conflicts between partners
    

## Quick Comparison

**Komplementär**  
→ manages the company  
→ unlimited liability

**Kommanditist**  
→ primarily provides capital  
→ limited liability  
→ generally no ordinary management authority

**Key idea:** A KG combines a **managing, fully liable partner** with one or more **capital-providing partners whose liability is limited**.
