#BWM #Business #LegalForms #P28
English: unlimited partnership

Minimum of two fully liable partners
They have to sign a business contract saying:
- rulings on 
	- various aspects on how to run the company
	- how to split the profit
	- possible orally -> problem in court

Has to register in the [[Company Register]] on day 1
register
Easier to get a loan

Liability:
- unlimited liability
- also liable for your partner's debt
- each partner can do anything (can be limited in the contract)

Distrubution of Profit: according to the contract

Taxation: non-taxable entity according to EStG, profits will just be split across business owners
	=> individual owners are getting taxed

Pros:
- No minimum capital required
- Business licenses can be sufficed by other owners
Cons:
- Liability
- Major risk of conflict
- Partners are not allowed to run the same businesses:
	=> If they're running a taxi company the partners can't run a sole trader taxi company
	=> To protect the partner from "cheating" on each other
