P.39 
The four company forms:
- GmbH (private limited company):
	- capital comes together instead of people
	- minimum capital 10.000 euro
		- only 50% -> 5.000 euro when you start
	- general assembly: people come together
	- CEO can be bound by the general assembly 

Personal experience by teach: don't look at the taxation aspect in the company
- How much will it close the company (closing costs)
- GmbH easy to sell
- Cooperations can take years to close

Process to start (not in the book):
1. Set up a contract (can be done alone nowadays): notary office inquiry to setup a GmbH contract -> pretty straight-forward
2. Bank -> show contract -> open an account -> pay in 5000 euro minimum
3. Notary office and they will handle all the documents, notation of the company and if they are fine with everything with your company it will be registered.


Cons:
- you have to go to the notary office, costs more
- mandatory double-sided accounting since day 1
	- tax-advisor: more expensive
- ...

P.44
Aktiengesellschaft (AG)

Listed at the vienna stock exchange. Can go to the stock market. Very similar to the GmbH

Aktionaere (shareholders) -> provide capital
minimum 70.000 EUR


major differences to GmbH:
- a stock can be traded like any product in AG
	- GmbH shares can't be without complexity
- general assembly has limited influence on the board of directors
	- officers can say no to many things listed in AG-Gesetz
